Who is this for?

This scorecard is for technical founders, CTOs and Heads of Engineering at UK healthtech companies running production workloads on AWS.

Especially if your application engineers have gradually picked up responsibility for infrastructure, security, deployments and reliability alongside building the product.

What will you assess?
  • Infrastructure ownership
  • Infrastructure as Code
  • IAM and security
  • CI/CD and deployments
  • Observability
  • Backup and recovery
  • Operational resilience
This isn't an audit

This scorecard is a quick diagnostic, not a compliance assessment or technical audit.

It won't tell you whether you're DTAC or DSPT compliant. It isn't a penetration test, AWS Well-Architected Review or substitute for a proper architecture assessment.

It's designed to do one thing: help you see where your AWS production setup may deserve a closer look.

I use this information to:

I will use your work email address to send the scorecard results you requested.
